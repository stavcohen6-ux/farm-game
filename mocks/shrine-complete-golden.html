Other notes

  • Burn immunity: exclude maxed shrines from pickBurnableShrine. If every shrine with progress is maxed, keep today’s lucky line (“wrath fades”).
  • Differentiate from blessing: blessing = temporary amber rim; complete = permanent gold tint (A/B/C). Avoid making complete look like a permanent blessing.
  • My lean: A (soft hue) or C (figure only). Both read “finished” without fighting Soft B colors or the blessing rim. B if you want it louder.
  • Skip for now: sparkles, checkmarks, crown badges — clutter at corner size and fight the “no shrine pulse” rule.
